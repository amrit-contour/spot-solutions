What the service is

Curate helps open-mic curators sort spot requests they receive on WhatsApp, build lineups and send replies. Comedy helps comics keep a roster of rooms, watch the WhatsApp groups they choose for spot offers, send spot asks to curators and keep track of their spots. The server is the program on your own computer that does the reading and sending. Spot alerts depend on what others post and on your server being online; they are never a guarantee that a spot exists or is still free.
